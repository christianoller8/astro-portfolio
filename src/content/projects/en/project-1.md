## The Challenge

The client needed to move from a physical store to a robust digital platform that could manage inventory in real time, process secure payments and deliver a smooth mobile experience. The main challenge was integrating a geolocation system to calculate zero-kilometer shipping.

## My Role

As Lead Developer, I was responsible for:

* Designing the database architecture in **PostgreSQL**.
* Implementing the payment flow with the **Stripe** API.
* Building reusable components with **React** and **Tailwind**.

## Result

The platform launch led to a **150% increase in sales** during the first quarter. Performance optimization (Lighthouse score of 98) kept the bounce rate to a minimum.

### Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Next.js 14, Framer Motion.
* **Backend:** Node.js, Prisma ORM.
* **Infrastructure:** Vercel, AWS S3.
